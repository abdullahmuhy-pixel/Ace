Managing exam stress

A little stress sharpens attention. Too much makes it hard to start. These habits help most students.

What to do

  1. Sleep is part of revision. Memory is stored overnight, so cutting sleep to study usually backfires.
  2. Break the work into small tasks that fit in 25 minutes. Starting is the hardest part.
  3. Move for ten minutes between sessions. Walking is enough.
  4. Talk to someone you trust. If worry is affecting your sleep or daily life for weeks, speak to a teacher, parent or a health professional.
