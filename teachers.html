What you get

The tools of a school, sized for your classes.

🔑A class in a minute

Name a class, get a six-letter join code and share it on WhatsApp or the board. Students join from their own accounts.

📝Set Ace work

Assign a topic quiz, a narrated lesson or a full timed mock from the Ace question bank. It is marked for you and the score lands in your gradebook.

✍️Your own tasks too

Written homework, reading, video or links, with due dates, submissions, rubrics and feedback.

📊See who is stuck

Each student's activity, scores by topic, streaks and the topics the class finds hardest.

📅Register and gradebook

Take attendance, record tests with weights, and use the continuous-assessment markbook for your country.

🗺️Curriculum pacing

Every module and topic of the syllabus your students sit, ticked off as you teach it.

⚡Live quiz

Run a live class quiz on any screen; students answer on their phones.

💬Messages and notices

Message a student, or post a notice to the whole class.

✨AI lesson planner

A lesson plan, worksheet or practice paper for any topic in seconds, ready to edit.

📄Reports for parents

Progress reports and report cards you can print, plus a family view parents open with a code.

Students stay in charge of their learning

Joining a class never takes anything away.

  • Students keep one account for their own study and every class they join.
  • You see a student's work only while they are in your class. They can leave at any time.
  • Parents get a read-only view only when the student shares it.

Running a centre with several teachers?

Add teachers to your workspace and give each one their classes. Centres with many students can ask for a quote.
