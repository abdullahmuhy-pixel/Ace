Grading and rules

Know the scale.

  • Grading. WASSCE/NECO: A1 to F9 (credit = C6 or better). UTME: 0 to 400.
  • Levels covered. SS1 – SS3
  • Currency in questions. ₦
  • Data protection. Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)
Worked example · Mathematics

Number bases and conversions

Convert 45 (base ten) to base 2.

45 ÷ 2 = 22 remainder 1

22 ÷ 2 = 11 remainder 0

11 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 1

5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1

2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0

1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading the remainders upwards: 45₁₀ = 101101₂.

Check: 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45 ✓

Questions

Common questions about Nigeria.

Which exams can I prepare for in Nigeria with Ace Education?

WASSCE and NECO SSCE for school leavers, and JAMB UTME for university admission. Ace Education covers WASSCE, UTME and NECO in Nigeria, with 63 subjects and 5,753 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for WASSCE, UTME and NECO in Nigeria?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which WASSCE subjects are covered in Nigeria?

Mathematics, English Language, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Government, Commerce, Financial Accounting, Civic Education, Literature in English, Geography, Information and Communication Technology, History and 8 more.

How is WASSCE graded in Nigeria?

WASSCE/NECO: A1 to F9 (credit = C6 or better). UTME: 0 to 400. Levels covered: SS1 – SS3.

Can I start studying for Nigeria exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
