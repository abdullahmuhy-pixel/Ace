About

Exam preparation that fits the exam you will actually sit.

Students across West Africa, East and Southern Africa, Europe and North America prepare for very different exams. Most study tools treat them as one. We build for each.

🎯Local first

Every country gets its own exam bodies, subject list, currency and examples. We do not translate one syllabus over and over.

🔍Honest about quality

New content says "needs review" until a teacher signs it off. We would rather show you the status than hide it.

🧮Numbers checked by code

Worked answers in maths, science, economics and accounting are recomputed by a program before they go in.

🔒Privacy first

We collect only what the service needs, we do not sell data, and deletion is available on request.

📱Built for phones

A web page that works in a mobile browser with no install, light enough for limited data.

🧘Whole student

Wellbeing sits next to marks. Exam prep should not cost students their sleep.

How a subject is made

From syllabus to student.

  1. Take the topic list from the exam body's published syllabus and group it into modules.
  2. Write the notes: overview, key terms, explanation, formulas, method, worked example, mistakes, real-world link, strategy and recap.
  3. Write at least five questions per topic with a wrong-answer explanation for each option that matters.
  4. Recompute every number in code, and test for duplicate options, answer positions and leftover content from other countries.
  5. Mark everything "needs review" until a subject teacher signs it off.
