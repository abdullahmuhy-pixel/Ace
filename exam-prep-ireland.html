Worked example · Mathematics

Sets and Venn diagrams

In a class of 50 students, 30 play football, 22 play basketball and 8 play both. How many play neither?

Both = 8

Football only = 30 − 8 = 22

Basketball only = 22 − 8 = 14

Inside the circles: 22 + 8 + 14 = 44

Neither = 50 − 44 = 6

6 students play neither.

Check: n(F ∪ B) = 30 + 22 − 8 = 44, and 50 − 44 = 6 ✓

Questions

Common questions about Ireland.

Which exams can I prepare for in Ireland with Ace Education?

The Leaving Certificate, run by the State Examinations Commission. Ace Education covers Leaving Certificate in Ireland, with 18 subjects and 1,664 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for Leaving Certificate in Ireland?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which Leaving Certificate subjects are covered in Ireland?

Physics, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, English, Geography, Irish, History, Economics, Accounting, Business, Computer Science, Applied Mathematics, Agricultural Science and 4 more.

How is Leaving Certificate graded in Ireland?

H1 to H8 (Higher) and O1 to O8 (Ordinary), turned into CAO points. Levels covered: 5th – 6th Year.

Can I start studying for Ireland exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
