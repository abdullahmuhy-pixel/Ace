Features

Everything in the app, and exactly where it stands.

20 features are live. Others are marked "Backend ready" or "Planned" so nobody has to guess what works today.

📘Topic notes

Live

An overview, key terms, a three-part explanation, formulas, a method, a worked example, common mistakes, a real-world link, exam strategy and a recap for every topic.

✅Topic quizzes

Live

Five questions at a time from the topic bank, scored at the end, with the working shown for every answer.

🔍Topic question bank

Live

Open any topic and practise every question written for it, or draw a random ten for the subject.

🃏Flashcards

Live

Curated definition and formula cards for each subject, plus cards made from each topic's questions.

⏱️Timed mock exams

Live

A paper drawn evenly from every module, with a countdown, a score by module and the topics to revise.

🤖Ask Ace

Live

An AI study buddy that knows which subject, topic and exam the student is on and explains step by step.

🔥Daily challenge and streaks

Live

Five questions a day, XP, and a streak that builds as the student keeps coming back.

🌍Thirteen countries, one app

Live

Switch country and the exams, subjects, currency and examples change with it. Spain also switches the interface to Spanish.

🎓Choose your subjects

Live

Students pick the subjects they actually take, and the app shows only those.

♿Accessibility settings

Live

Dark mode, high-contrast mode and a dyslexia-friendly font.

✍️What my teacher taught today

Backend ready

The student types what was covered in class. Ace returns a summary, key terms, a worked example and practice questions, and schedules revision.

🔁Spaced revision

Backend ready

Short reviews after 1, 3, 7, 21 and 45 days. A wrong answer brings the topic back tomorrow.

🗺️Road map to graduation

Live

From lower secondary to career, with the next steps at each stage and a saved goal for course and university.

🏛️University explorer

Live

Courses and how admission works at leading universities in each of the thirteen countries, with links to each official site.

🧮GPA, citation and focus tools

Live

5.0, 4.0 and UK grade calculators with a "what do I need" planner, an APA and Harvard citation builder, and a focus timer.

📝Lecture-note study packs

Backend ready

Paste your own lecture notes and get a summary, flashcards and practice questions.

🎓Certificate courses

Backend ready

33 short courses: study skills, coding (Python, JavaScript, SQL, HTML and CSS, Git), digital safety, AI literacy, business and money, public speaking and more. Quizzes are graded on the server and certificates can be verified by anyone.

💬Moderated study groups

Backend ready

Groups for a class, a school or a campus. No links or phone numbers, member reports, automatic hiding and teacher oversight for school groups.

👪Share one plan with up to 3 others

Backend ready

One payer plus three members, each with their own account and privacy. Friends, classmates or siblings.

🧑‍🏫Staff dashboard

Live

Attendance, marks, tasks, notices, messages, live quizzes, parent digest, moderation, school settings and platform admin in one place.

⚡Live quiz

Backend ready

A teacher starts a quiz, students join with a code and answer on their phones. The answer key never reaches the student.

🔔Reminders and notices by push

Backend ready

Daily revision reminders and teacher notices delivered to the phone.

🏠Family view

Backend ready

A private link parents can open, with no account, to see streak, weekly practice and course progress. The student can stop it any time.

🆓Open student sign-up

Live

Any student can create an account, choose a country, level and exam, and save progress across devices. No school needed.

🔐Username sign-in

Live

Students sign in with a username. No email needed, so a school can create accounts for a whole class.

📊Weak Topic Radar

Live

Finds the topics a student keeps getting wrong from their own quiz answers and offers a practice quiz for each.

🧠Tutor Mode

Live

An AI that guides with questions and hints instead of giving the answer.

✍️Essay grader and mark scheme checker

Live

Paste an essay or an answer and get an estimated mark, strengths and fixes. AI marking is an estimate, not an official mark.

📄Question generator

Live

Paste your notes and get a practice paper with answers.

📐Formula sheet

Planned

Every formula for the subject in one place, by topic.

🗓️Study planner and timetable

Planned

Exam dates, daily goals and a countdown that follow the national exam timetable.

📝My notes

Planned

Write notes next to each topic and keep them with the subject.
