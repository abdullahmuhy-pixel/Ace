Getting the most from Ace Education

Ace Education is built around a simple loop: learn a topic, test it, then sit a mock.

What to do

  1. Open a subject and pick the first topic you are shaky on.
  2. Read the notes, then take the five-question quiz.
  3. Use Ask Ace when a step does not make sense. Ask it to explain, not to just give the answer.
  4. When you have covered a module, sit the timed mock and read the module breakdown.
  5. Come back every day for the daily challenge. A short daily habit beats a long weekly session.
