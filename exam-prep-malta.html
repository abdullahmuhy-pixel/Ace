Grading and rules

Know the scale.

  • Grading. SEC: Paper II is sat at Levels 1–2 or 2–3, and grade 1 is the highest. Matriculation: grades A to E; AM gives 30 to 6 points and IM 10 to 2 points.
  • Levels covered. Year 9 – Sixth Form
  • Currency in questions. €
  • Data protection. GDPR
Worked example · Mathematics

Sets and Venn diagrams

In a class of 50 students, 30 play football, 22 play basketball and 8 play both. How many play neither?

Both = 8

Football only = 30 − 8 = 22

Basketball only = 22 − 8 = 14

Inside the circles: 22 + 8 + 14 = 44

Neither = 50 − 44 = 6

6 students play neither.

Check: n(F ∪ B) = 30 + 22 − 8 = 44, and 50 − 44 = 6 ✓

Questions

Common questions about Malta.

Which exams can I prepare for in Malta with Ace Education?

The Secondary Education Certificate (SEC) at the end of Year 11, and the Matriculation Certificate after two years of sixth form, both set by MATSEC at the University of Malta. Ace Education covers SEC and Matriculation in Malta, with 28 subjects and 3,200 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for SEC and Matriculation in Malta?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which SEC subjects are covered in Malta?

Mathematics, English Language, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computing, Business Studies, Economics, Accounting, Geography, History, Maltese.

How is SEC graded in Malta?

SEC: Paper II is sat at Levels 1–2 or 2–3, and grade 1 is the highest. Matriculation: grades A to E; AM gives 30 to 6 points and IM 10 to 2 points. Levels covered: Year 9 – Sixth Form.

Can I start studying for Malta exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
