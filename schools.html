Getting a school started

Five steps.

  1. Request a quote and tell us the exams and number of learners.
  2. We create your school and give you a school code.
  3. Invite teachers, or let them join with the code.
  4. Add students one by one or by bulk import. Each gets a username, so no email is needed.
  5. Choose the subjects each class sees and start practising.
