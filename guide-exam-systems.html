How the exam systems compare

The countries in Ace Education test students in very different ways. Here is the quick difference.

What to do

  1. West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana, Sierra Leone, The Gambia, Liberia): WASSCE grades run A1 to F9, and a credit is C6 or better.
  2. Kenya: KCSE gives a mean grade A to E, with 12 points for A.
  3. Zambia: ECZ grades run from 1 (best) to 9.
  4. UK: GCSE uses 9 to 1 and A-Level uses A* to E.
  5. Canada: marks in Ontario are percentages.
  6. Spain: the EBAU is marked 0 to 14 including the voluntary phase.
  7. Ireland: Leaving Certificate grades H1 to H8 and O1 to O8 turn into CAO points.
  8. South Africa: the NSC reports achievement levels 1 to 7, where level 7 is 80 to 100%.
  9. Uganda: UCE grades run 1 (best) to 9, and UACE gives principal passes A to F.
  10. Tanzania: CSEE uses divisions I to IV and 0, and ACSEE grades run A to F.
  11. Malta: SEC Paper II is sat at Levels 1–2 or 2–3, and grade 1 is the highest.
  12. Zimbabwe: ZIMSEC O Level grades run A to E with C or better a pass, and A Level grades A to E give 5 to 1 points.
  13. France: marks out of 20; the bac needs a 10/20 average, with mentions from 12, 14 and 16.
  14. Botswana: BGCSE grades run A* to G, with U for ungraded.
