Number bases and conversions

Convert 45 (base ten) to base 2.

45 ÷ 2 = 22 remainder 1

22 ÷ 2 = 11 remainder 0

11 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 1

5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1

2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0

1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading the remainders upwards: 45₁₀ = 101101₂.

Check: 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45 ✓

Questions

Common questions about Ghana.

Which exams can I prepare for in Ghana with Ace Education?

WASSCE at the end of senior high school and the BECE at the end of junior high school. Ace Education covers WASSCE and BECE in Ghana, with 33 subjects and 2,877 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for WASSCE and BECE in Ghana?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which WASSCE subjects are covered in Ghana?

Core Mathematics, English Language, Social Studies, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, Commerce, Financial Accounting, Integrated Science, Elective Mathematics, Agricultural Science, Literature in English, Geography and 9 more.

How is WASSCE graded in Ghana?

WASSCE: A1 to F9. BECE: grades 1 to 9 in each subject. Levels covered: SHS 1 – SHS 3.

Can I start studying for Ghana exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
