Number bases and conversions

Convert 45 (base ten) to base 2.

45 ÷ 2 = 22 remainder 1

22 ÷ 2 = 11 remainder 0

11 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 1

5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1

2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0

1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading the remainders upwards: 45₁₀ = 101101₂.

Check: 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45 ✓

Which exams can I prepare for in The Gambia with Ace Education?

WASSCE at Grade 12 and the GABECE at the end of basic education. Ace Education covers WASSCE and GABECE in The Gambia, with 35 subjects and 3,107 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for WASSCE and GABECE in The Gambia?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which WASSCE subjects are covered in The Gambia?

How is WASSCE graded in The Gambia?

WASSCE: A1 to F9. Levels covered: Grade 10 – 12.
