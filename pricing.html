Questions

About plans and payment.

Do I need to pay to use Ace?

No. The notes, lessons, quizzes, flashcards, question bank and study tools are free for everyone, with no time limit. A plan only adds more AI help, unlimited mocks and verified certificates.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Your account moves to the free plan. You keep all your progress, streaks and notes. Nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.

What is a term?

A termly plan runs for about three months, which covers a school term.

How does the group plan work?

One person pays and invites up to three others from Share my plan. Everyone keeps their own account and progress.

My school has a licence. Do I need a plan?

No. If your school or your tutor has a licence, you get the plan features through it.
