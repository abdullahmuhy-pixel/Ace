Number bases and conversions

Convert 45 (base ten) to base 2.

45 ÷ 2 = 22 remainder 1

22 ÷ 2 = 11 remainder 0

11 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 1

5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1

2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0

1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Questions

Common questions about Liberia.

Which exams can I prepare for in Liberia with Ace Education?

WASSCE at the end of senior high school (Grade 12), administered in Liberia by WAEC. Ace Education covers WASSCE in Liberia, with 9 subjects and 1,222 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for WASSCE in Liberia?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which WASSCE subjects are covered in Liberia?

English Language, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Economics, Geography, History, Literature in English.

How is WASSCE graded in Liberia?

WASSCE grades and pass rules are set by WAEC; check the current rules for Liberia. Levels covered: Grade 10 – 12.

Can I start studying for Liberia exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
