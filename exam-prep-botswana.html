Worked example · Mathematics

Sets and Venn diagrams

In a class of 40 students, 25 study Physics, 18 study Chemistry and 8 study both. How many study neither?

Union = 25 + 18 − 8 = 35.

Neither = 40 − 35 = 5.

Questions

Common questions about Botswana.

Which exams can I prepare for in Botswana with Ace Education?

The Botswana General Certificate of Secondary Education (BGCSE) at the end of Form 5, set by the Botswana Examinations Council (BEC). The Junior Certificate (JCE) comes at the end of Form 3. Ace Education covers BGCSE in Botswana, with 6 subjects and 670 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for BGCSE in Botswana?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which BGCSE subjects are covered in Botswana?

Computer Studies, Mathematics, English Language, Biology, Chemistry, Physics.

How is BGCSE graded in Botswana?

BGCSE grades run from A* (highest) to G; U means ungraded. Levels covered: Form 1 – Form 5.

Can I start studying for Botswana exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
