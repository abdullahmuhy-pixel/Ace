Terms of use.

Plain-language summary. This is a working draft and a lawyer should review it before you rely on it.

Using Ace Education

  1. Ace Education is a study aid. It does not replace your teacher, your school or the official syllabus and timetable of your exam body.
  2. Look after your password and do not share your account. Tell us if you think someone else has used it.
  3. Be kind in study groups and messages. We may remove content and accounts that break this rule.

Accuracy

  1. Notes and questions are written for the app and checked, but some items are still marked "needs review". Always confirm important facts against your syllabus and teacher.
  2. Answers from Ace, the AI study buddy, can be wrong. Check them, especially for marks and exam rules.

Exam bodies

  1. WAEC, NECO, JAMB, KNEC, ECZ, UNEB, NECTA, Umalusi, AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC and other exam bodies are named only to say which exam a subject follows. Ace Education is not affiliated with them.

Schools and accounts

  1. Schools are responsible for their staff and student accounts. A school or student can ask us to delete an account at any time.
  2. We may change or stop parts of the service. We will try to give notice of big changes.

Questions about these terms? Use the contact page.
