Worked example · Mathematics

Sets and Venn diagrams

In a class of 50 students, 30 play football, 22 play basketball and 8 play both. How many play neither?

Both = 8

Football only = 30 − 8 = 22

Basketball only = 22 − 8 = 14

Inside the circles: 22 + 8 + 14 = 44

Neither = 50 − 44 = 6

6 students play neither.

Check: n(F ∪ B) = 30 + 22 − 8 = 44, and 50 − 44 = 6 ✓

Questions

Common questions about Canada.

Which exams can I prepare for in Canada with Ace Education?

The Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD), from the Ontario Ministry of Education. Ace Education covers OSSD and Provincial diploma exams in Canada, with 21 subjects and 1,869 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for OSSD and Provincial diploma exams in Canada?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which OSSD subjects are covered in Canada?

Physics, Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry, English, Canadian History, Advanced Functions, Calculus and Vectors, Data Management, Computer Science, Geography, Economics, Law, Psychology and 1 more.

How is OSSD graded in Canada?

Percentage marks, with Ontario's achievement levels 1 to 4. Levels covered: Grade 11 – 12.

Can I start studying for Canada exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
