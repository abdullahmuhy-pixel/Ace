Worked example · Mathematics

Exponents and surds

Simplify (2ⁿ⁺² − 2ⁿ) ÷ 2ⁿ⁺¹.

Write 2ⁿ⁺² = 2ⁿ·4 and 2ⁿ⁺¹ = 2ⁿ·2.

Numerator: 2ⁿ(4 − 1) = 3·2ⁿ.

Divide: 3·2ⁿ ÷ (2·2ⁿ) = 3/2.

Questions

Common questions about South Africa.

Which exams can I prepare for in South Africa with Ace Education?

The National Senior Certificate (NSC, "matric"), set by the Department of Basic Education and quality-assured by Umalusi. Ace Education covers NSC in South Africa, with 23 subjects and 2,140 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for NSC in South Africa?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which NSC subjects are covered in South Africa?

Mathematics, Mathematical Literacy, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Accounting, Business Studies, Economics, Geography, History, English Home Language, English First Additional Language, Agricultural Sciences, Information Technology, Computer Applications Technology and 9 more.

How is NSC graded in South Africa?

Level 1 to 7: 7 is 80 to 100%, 6 is 70 to 79%, 5 is 60 to 69%, 4 is 50 to 59%, 3 is 40 to 49%, 2 is 30 to 39% and 1 is 0 to 29%. Levels covered: Grade 10 – 12.

Can I start studying for South Africa exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
