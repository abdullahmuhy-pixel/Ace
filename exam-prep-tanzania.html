Sets and Venn diagrams

In a class of 40 students, 25 study Physics, 18 study Chemistry and 8 study both. How many study neither?

Union = 25 + 18 − 8 = 35.

Neither = 40 − 35 = 5.

Questions

Common questions about Tanzania.

Which exams can I prepare for in Tanzania with Ace Education?

The Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE, Form 4) and the Advanced Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (ACSEE, Form 6), set by NECTA. Ace Education covers CSEE and ACSEE in Tanzania, with 28 subjects and 3,020 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for CSEE and ACSEE in Tanzania?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which CSEE subjects are covered in Tanzania?

Basic Mathematics, English Language, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History, Commerce, Bible Knowledge, Islamic Knowledge, Agriculture, Information and Computer Studies, Literature in English, Kiswahili.

How is CSEE graded in Tanzania?

CSEE: divisions I to IV and 0 (grades A to F). ACSEE: grades A to F and S, with points for university. Levels covered: Form 1 – 6.

Can I start studying for Tanzania exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
