Privacy

How we handle student data.

Plain-language summary. This is a working draft and a lawyer in each country should review it before launch.

What we collect

  1. Account details a school or student gives us: a username, a display name, class and school.
  2. Study activity: which topics were opened, quiz answers and scores, streaks and XP.
  3. Messages a student sends to Ask Ace, so the tutor can answer in context.

What we do not do

  1. We do not sell personal data.
  2. We do not show advertising to students.
  3. We do not need a student's email address to sign in when the school uses usernames.

Control and deletion

  1. A student, parent or school can ask for data to be deleted. The app has a "delete my data" option.
  2. Schools only see their own students. The database separates schools by design.
