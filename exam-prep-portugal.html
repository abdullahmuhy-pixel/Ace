Exams

The Exames Nacionais do Ensino Secundário, set and run by EduQA (the education quality and evaluation institute that took over from IAVE in 2026) through the Júri Nacional de Exames (JNE). Students on the cursos científico-humanísticos sit three exams to finish secondary school: Português (12.º ano) and two more from their course (or one and Filosofia). The exam counts for 25% of the final grade in that subject, and exams also serve as provas de ingresso for higher education.

Exames Nacionais

EduQA e JNE (Júri Nacional de Exames)

Taken in June (1.ª fase) and July (2.ª fase) of the 11.º or 12.º ano. Each paper is marked out of 200 points. Português (639) is compulsory for all students on the cursos científico-humanísticos; the most common other exams are Matemática A, Física e Química A, Biologia e Geologia, História A, Geografia A, Economia A and Filosofia.

Which exams can I prepare for in Portugal with Ace Education?

The Exames Nacionais do Ensino Secundário, set and run by EduQA (the education quality and evaluation institute that took over from IAVE in 2026) through the Júri Nacional de Exames (JNE). Students on the cursos científico-humanísticos sit three exams to finish secondary school: Português (12.º ano) and two more from their course (or one and Filosofia). Ace Education covers Exames Nacionais in Portugal, with 4 subjects and 505 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for Exames Nacionais in Portugal?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which Exames Nacionais subjects are covered in Portugal?

Português (639), Matemática A (635), Física e Química A (715), Biologia e Geologia (702).

How is Exames Nacionais graded in Portugal?

Final grades are on a 0 to 20 scale and 10 is a pass. Exams are marked from 0 to 200 points; provas de ingresso usually need at least 95. Levels covered: 10.º – 12.º ano.

Can I start studying for Portugal exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
