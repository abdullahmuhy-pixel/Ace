Calculus I (135 questions)

  • Functions and limits: Functions, domains and inverses, Limits and the limit laws, One-sided limits, infinite limits and asymptotes, Continuity and the Intermediate Value Theorem
  • The derivative: The derivative as a limit, Power, constant and sum rules, Product and quotient rules, The chain rule
  • More derivatives: Derivatives of trigonometric functions, Exponential and logarithmic derivatives, Implicit differentiation, Related rates
  • Applications of the derivative: Critical points and extreme values, The Mean Value Theorem and the shape of a graph, Optimisation, L'Hôpital's rule and linear approximation
  • Integration: Antiderivatives and indefinite integrals, Riemann sums and the definite integral, The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, Integration by substitution, Area between curves

Linear Algebra (114 questions)

  • Systems of linear equations: Linear systems and their solutions, Gaussian elimination, Reduced row echelon form and rank, Homogeneous systems and parametric solutions
  • Matrix algebra: Matrix operations, Transpose and special matrices, The inverse of a matrix, Matrix equations and invertibility
  • Determinants: Computing determinants, Properties of determinants, Cramer's rule, area and volume
  • Vectors and vector spaces: Vectors, dot product, length and angle, Span and linear independence, Subspaces, basis and dimension, Null space, column space and rank–nullity
  • Linear transformations and eigenvalues: Linear transformations and their matrices, Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, Diagonalisation, Orthogonality and projections

Probability and Statistics (120 questions)

  • Probability: Sample spaces and the rules of probability, Conditional probability and independence, Bayes' theorem, Counting: permutations and combinations
  • Random variables and distributions: Discrete random variables: expectation and variance, The binomial distribution, The Poisson distribution, Continuous random variables
  • Normal distribution, sampling and confidence intervals: The normal distribution and z-scores, Sampling distributions and the Central Limit Theorem, Confidence intervals for a mean, Confidence intervals for a proportion
  • Hypothesis testing and regression: Hypothesis testing: p-values and errors, Tests for a mean and a proportion, The chi-square test of independence, Correlation and linear regression
