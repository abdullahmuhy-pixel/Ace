Grading and rules

Know the scale.

  • Grading. UCE: grades 1 (best) to 9 and a division. UACE: principal passes A to F and subsidiary O or F.
  • Levels covered. S1 – S6
  • Currency in questions. USh

Sets and Venn diagrams

In a class of 40 students, 25 study Physics, 18 study Chemistry and 8 study both. How many study neither?

Union = 25 + 18 − 8 = 35.

Neither = 40 − 35 = 5.

Questions

Common questions about Uganda.

Which exams can I prepare for in Uganda with Ace Education?

The Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE, S4) and the Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE, S6), set by UNEB. Ace Education covers UCE and UACE in Uganda, with 28 subjects and 2,975 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for UCE and UACE in Uganda?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which UCE subjects are covered in Uganda?

Mathematics, English Language, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Geography, History and Political Education, Commerce, Christian Religious Education, Islamic Religious Education, Agriculture, Computer Studies, Literature in English, Kiswahili and 1 more.

How is UCE graded in Uganda?

UCE: grades 1 (best) to 9 and a division. UACE: principal passes A to F and subsidiary O or F. Levels covered: S1 – S6.

Can I start studying for Uganda exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
