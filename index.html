The student app

Learn a topic, test it, then sit a mock.

Every subject is split into modules and topics that follow the exam syllabus. Each topic works the same way, so students always know what to do next.

📘

Topic notes

An overview, key terms, a three-part explanation, formulas, a method, a worked example, common mistakes, a real-world link, exam strategy and a recap for every topic.

✅

Topic quizzes

Five questions at a time from the topic bank, scored at the end, with the working shown for every answer.

🔍

Topic question bank

Open any topic and practise every question written for it, or draw a random ten for the subject.

🃏

Flashcards

Curated definition and formula cards for each subject, plus cards made from each topic's questions.

⏱️

Timed mock exams

A paper drawn evenly from every module, with a countdown, a score by module and the topics to revise.

🤖

Ask Ace

An AI study buddy that knows which subject, topic and exam the student is on and explains step by step.

Number bases and conversions

Convert 45 (base ten) to base 2.

45 ÷ 2 = 22 remainder 1

22 ÷ 2 = 11 remainder 0

11 ÷ 2 = 5 remainder 1

5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1

2 ÷ 2 = 1 remainder 0

1 ÷ 2 = 0 remainder 1

Reading the remainders upwards: 45₁₀ = 101101₂.

Check: 32 + 8 + 4 + 1 = 45 ✓

How content is made

Written from the syllabus, checked by code, then by teachers.

📑

Written from the syllabus

Topic lists follow the published subject syllabus of each exam body. Notes and questions are written fresh for the app, not copied from past papers or textbooks.

🧮

Every number is recomputed

Answers in mathematics, physics, chemistry, economics and accounting are worked out by a program before they go in. Duplicate options and answer positions are checked as well.

🏷️

Labelled until a teacher signs off

New material carries a "needs review" label until a subject teacher approves it. That review is the next stage, and the label tells students where each item stands.

Questions

Straight answers.

Which exams does Ace Education cover today?

More than twenty exams across fifteen countries, including: WASSCE, NECO and UTME in Nigeria; WASSCE and BECE in Ghana; WASSCE, BECE and NPSE in Sierra Leone; WASSCE and GABECE in The Gambia; WASSCE in Liberia; KCSE in Kenya; ECZ Grade 12 in Zambia; GCSE and A-Level in the UK; the OSSD and Alberta diploma exams in Canada; the PAU (EBAU) in Spain with regional content; the Leaving Certificate in Ireland; the NSC in South Africa; UCE and UACE in Uganda; CSEE and ACSEE in Tanzania; and the SEC in Malta. The Countries page shows exactly which subjects each one has.

Are the questions copied from past papers?

No. Topic lists follow each exam body's published syllabus, and the notes and questions are written for the app. We do not reproduce past papers or textbooks.

Has a teacher checked the content?

Every note and question is checked by code (answers recomputed, duplicates and answer positions tested) and read through for local accuracy. A subject teacher's sign-off is the next stage, and until it happens the item carries a "needs review" label. The Countries page shows the review status for each country.

How do you make sure the content is local?

Each country has its own file with its own exams, subjects, currency, tax rate and examples. A Kenyan student sees shillings and 16% VAT, a student in Ireland sees euro and 23% VAT, and a student in Canada sees 13% HST and 120 V mains.

Is it free for students?

Anyone can create a student account and start practising. Schools and centres agree a plan with us for class tools and extra seats. See the Quote page.
