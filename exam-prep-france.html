Français · Seconde – Terminale

🇫🇷 France

France's content is written in French, and so is the app's interface for this country. Épreuves anticipées: mathématiques (specialty, non-specialty and technological papers) and français (general and technological tracks, with the set works for the 2027 session). Bac général: philosophy, the specialty subjects including mathématiques, physique-chimie, SVT, SES, HGGSP, HLP, NSI, sciences de l'ingénieur, LLCER (anglais, espagnol, allemand), AMC, LLCA latin and grec, the arts specialties (arts plastiques, cinéma, musique, théâtre, histoire des arts, danse) with their set works, biologie-écologie and EPPCS, plus enseignement scientifique, histoire-géographie, EMC, modern languages and Grand oral preparation. Bac technologique: philosophy and the specialties of STMG, STI2D, STL, ST2S, STD2A, STHR, S2TMD and the agricultural STAV.

Exams

The baccalauréat, general and technological tracks. Première students sit the épreuves anticipées (French, and from 2026 a written mathematics paper for every student); Terminale students sit philosophy, two specialty subjects and the Grand oral. Continuous assessment counts for 40% of the final mark.

Épreuves anticipées

Ministère de l'Éducation nationale

Papers taken at the end of Première: French (written and oral; commentary or dissertation in the general track, commentary or contraction and essay in the technological track) and, from the 2026 session, a two-hour mathematics paper without a calculator, with 6 points of automatismes and 14 points of exercises.

Grading and rules

Know the scale.

  • Grading. Marks are out of 20. The bac is passed with an average of 10/20; mentions start at 12 (assez bien), 14 (bien) and 16 (très bien).
  • Levels covered. Seconde – Terminale
  • Currency in questions. €
  • Data protection. RGPD
Worked example · Mathématiques (spécialité)

Calcul numérique et fractions

Calculer A = (1 + 1/2) / (1 − 1/4).

Numérateur : 1 + 1/2 = 3/2.

Dénominateur : 1 − 1/4 = 3/4.

A = 3/2 ÷ 3/4 = 3/2 × 4/3 = 12/6 = 2.

A = 2

2 × 3/4 = 3/2 ✓

Questions

Common questions about France.

Which exams can I prepare for in France with Ace Education?

The baccalauréat, general and technological tracks. Première students sit the épreuves anticipées (French, and from 2026 a written mathematics paper for every student); Terminale students sit philosophy, two specialty subjects and the Grand oral. Continuous assessment counts for 40% of the final mark. Ace Education covers Épreuves anticipées, Baccalauréat and Bac technologique in France, with 51 subjects and 3,820 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for Épreuves anticipées, Baccalauréat and Bac technologique in France?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which Épreuves anticipées subjects are covered in France?

Mathématiques (spécialité), Français, Mathématiques (sans spécialité), Français (voie technologique), Mathématiques (voie technologique).

How is Épreuves anticipées graded in France?

Marks are out of 20. The bac is passed with an average of 10/20; mentions start at 12 (assez bien), 14 (bien) and 16 (très bien). Levels covered: Seconde – Terminale.

Can I start studying for France exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
