Questions

Common questions about España.

Which exams can I prepare for in España with Ace Education?

The EBAU / PAU university-entrance exam after 2º Bachillerato. Ace Education covers EBAU / Selectividad in España, with 20 subjects and 1,475 practice questions in total.

How do I prepare for EBAU / Selectividad in España?

Open a subject, read the topic notes, take the five-question quiz, then sit a timed mock exam when you have covered a module. Ace Education shows your weakest topics so you know what to revise next.

Which EBAU / Selectividad subjects are covered in España?

Matemáticas II, Física, Química, Biología, Historia de España, Lengua Castellana y Literatura II, Inglés, Historia de la Filosofía, Matemáticas Aplicadas a las Ciencias Sociales II, Economía de la Empresa, Geografía, Dibujo Técnico II, Latín II, Geología y Ciencias Ambientales and 6 more.

How is EBAU / Selectividad graded in España?

EBAU: 0 to 14, from the general phase (0 to 10) plus the voluntary phase. Levels covered: 1º – 2º Bachillerato.

Can I start studying for España exams without a school code?

Yes. Anyone can create a student account on the sign-up page and choose their country, level and exam. Schools and centres can also join with a school code or ask us for a quote.
